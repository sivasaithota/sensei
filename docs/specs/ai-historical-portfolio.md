# Historical AI portfolio pilot

Build a research-only historical runner, with no paper/live order authority. Reuse the pinned raw-price momentum accounting engine without modifying its implementation. Replace formation selection with evidence-bounded AI target allocations; disable mechanical ATR sizing and trailing exits. Keep price-gap sizing caps, execution permissions, participation limits, slippage, ticks, charges, settlement, share availability and supported corporate actions. Existing resulting-security disposal policy is inherited; block a decision if unresolved resulting-security holdings cannot be represented in the AI packet.

Each decision sees cash, holdings, unavailable shares, receivables and a price/volume packet truncated at the decision date. Receivables contribute to equity but not spending power. Archive publication/availability at historical close is an explicit assumption, not authenticated historical capture. Do not supply future bars, future action outcomes, strategy ranks or benchmark results to models. Modern-model training hindsight remains possible.

Accept only validated AI decisions; failures abort the performance report rather than turning into cash decisions. Preserve every role artifact. Translate target quantities into next-session orders, preserve actual partial/non-fills, and carry the resulting account forward. Orders can be reduced by actual execution affordability/risk caps. Monthly decisions supersede prior pending allocations. Review horizons are recorded but are not automatically executed in this pilot.

Freeze the first empirical run before outputs: 2025-06-30 through 2025-08-29, June and July month-end decisions, top 20 stocks by strictly prior 60-session median turnover from dated tradable inception symbols. Require at least 21 bars, freeze universe, do not select by subsequent returns. The source panel has selection/survivorship limitations. Maximum 14 model calls, seven per decision. Use the existing ₹3 lakh accounting policy; model usage costs remain unknown/excluded.

Report AI net-of-execution-cost return, drawdown, fills, exposure and attribution; compare same dates to Nifty 500 gross TRI and a same-universe momentum control using frozen mechanics and the same accounting costs. Clearly distinguish this limited control from the full-universe frozen v8 result. Register implementation/source hashes and retain source-evidence identity. A failure records its reason without a headline AI return.

No-model replay verifies saved packet identity against regenerated evidence and account state. Tests must cover next-session fills, multiple decisions/holdings continuity, dividend receivables, failed models producing no result and deterministic replay including changed-account rejection.

## Operational recovery

The first empirical attempt timed out in Crowd Reader after 300 seconds, before any portfolio decision. Permit one separately recorded recovery attempt with medium CLI reasoning effort, the same dates/universe/accounting and no performance-based parameter changes. Reuse only a contiguous prefix of schema/citation-validated responses whose packet, role, system prompt, user prompt and schema match exactly. Preserve the original failed artifact and retain its digest/provider on every reused response. The per-attempt 14-call ceiling remains; reused roles make no new calls. A timed-out provider request may still consume usage, so total provider usage across attempts is not represented as 14 successful calls or zero cost.
