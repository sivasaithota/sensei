# Share-action classification: nine split candidates

Research date: 7 September 2026. Frozen-snapshot boundary supplied for this review: 4 September 2026. Scope: primary-source classification of the specified 2024–2026 events. No broker calls, credentials, purchases, or price-data changes were involved.

`total_share_ratio_numerator / total_share_ratio_denominator` means **total shares after the event per share before it**. A 4:1 bonus therefore has a total-share ratio of 5/1, whereas a subdivision from ₹10 to ₹2 has a ratio of 5/1. Ratios below follow the published share actions, not observed price or volume factors. All listed ex dates precede the supplied snapshot boundary.

## Bajaj Finance composition and timing

The company's 17 June 2025 clarification explicitly describes one ₹2 share becoming two ₹1 shares, followed by four bonus ₹1 shares for each existing ₹1 share. It identifies 16 June as the record date for both actions and 17 June as the deemed allotment date. Thus the split's **2/1** and bonus's **5/1** must be represented as separate events with the bonus applying to post-split units. [Company clarification](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BAJFINANCE_17062025130639_Clarificationletter.pdf).

NSE FAOP68547 explicitly publishes split factor 2, bonus factor 5, and their product 10, with ex/effective date 16 June 2025. The **10/1 composition** is consequently supported by explicit company terms and the exchange's published composition; it is not inferred from the candidate's observed endpoint volume factor. This does not authorize importing derivatives rounding or settlement rules into the cash-equity ledger. [NSE combined-action notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP68547.pdf).

The company's allotment notice confirms that 4,971,429,216 bonus shares of ₹1 were allotted on 17 June to eligible holders as of 16 June. It gives the resulting paid-up share count as 6,214,286,520. **Ex date, record date, and allotment date are different fields**; do not set the ex date to the allotment date or assume the additional bonus shares were already sellable at the opening of the ex date. This review has not established a separate bonus trading-availability date. [Company allotment notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BAJFINANCE_17062025163606_SEIntimation_Bonus_Allotment_June2025.pdf).

## Boundaries for the inventory

These are verified **event components**, not an exhaustive corporate-action history for any issuer. No absence of earlier or subsequent bonuses, subdivisions, demergers, rights, cash dividends, mergers, or other changes is certified. In particular, no observed endpoint factor should be promoted into a cumulative adjustment rule merely because it matches one event above.

Record the split and bonus classifications separately, preserve the dated ISIN lineage, and keep publication dates distinct from effective dates. These notices establish share-unit transformations; they do not establish Kite's complete adjustment chain, total-return prices, historical tradability of fractional quantities, or correct portfolio returns. Final validation still requires matching the dated event against raw sessions and the backtest's quantity/cash convention.
