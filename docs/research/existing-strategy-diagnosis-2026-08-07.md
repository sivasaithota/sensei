# Existing strategy diagnosis — 7 August 2026

## Decision

The current strategy set is **not ready for real capital**. Do not interpret
the strong latest-year result as confirmation: every combination fails at
least one longer-window or cost-stress cell, and the current data set is not a
point-in-time universe.

This diagnosis is research-only. It does not change lifecycle state, paper
positions, the scheduler, or the operational journal.

## Frozen experiment

- Initial capital: ₹300,000
- Windows: 252, 756 and 1,260 sessions (approximately 1, 3 and 5 years)
- Round-trip cost assumptions: 0.25%, 0.50% and 1.00%. Each is one total
  round-trip proxy applied to entry not a per-side rate; it is reserved at entry
  for conservative cash accounting. It does not yet separate taxes, fees,
  slippage and market impact.
- Portfolio rules: integer shares, cash only, 20% maximum position, 2% maximum
  initial risk per trade, five positions, one position per strategy and symbol
- Execution: signal at close, next-session open entry, stop-first resolution
  when a daily bar touches both stop and target
- Indicator warm-up: all available pre-window history; trades and equity only
  inside the requested evaluation window
- Ranking: number of positive window/cost cells, then median return, then worst
  drawdown. It is a diagnostic ordering, not an approval score.

The reproducible machine-readable artifact is generated by:

```bash
uv run sensei diagnose-strategies \
  --capital 300000 \
  --windows 252 756 1260 \
  --costs 0.25 0.50 1.00 \
  --report data/reports/strategy-diagnostic-matrix-300k.json
```

## Per-strategy conclusions

### Minervini breakout volume

Keep unchanged as the only provisional survivor. It remains positive in eight
of nine cells and misses only the three-year severe-cost cell (-0.32%). That is
promising, not sufficient: its latest-year result contains only six completed
trades, and it still needs point-in-time data, benchmarks, folds, regime and
liquidity attribution, and locked confirmation.

### Minervini trend template

Quarantine from new portfolio experiments. It is positive only in the five-year
base-cost cell and loses in all one- and three-year cells. Do not repair it by
tuning its stop, target or holding period on these results; any redesign must be
a new preregistered hypothesis.

### Gujral trend alignment

Quarantine. It loses in every cell, including -34.96% at base cost over five
years and -71.11% under severe costs. Its high trade count and cost sensitivity
indicate frequent low-edge entries rather than a temporary small-sample miss.

### Sadekar hammer confirmation

Freeze for causal diagnosis. Recent and three-year base-cost results are
positive, but the five-year result is negative at every cost and the median
cell is slightly negative. Investigate market regime, liquidity, and event
context before proposing a distinct replacement.

### Schwager pullback

Quarantine from new portfolio experiments. Its positive latest year does not
survive the three- or five-year windows. This confirms the earlier finding that
the generic momentum ranking selects poor pullback candidates and that the
strategy itself lacks stable portfolio expectancy under the current design.

## Data limitations that prevent promotion

The evaluation excludes symbols without a complete bar series in each window:
409/500 symbols for one year, 365/500 for three years and 337/500 for five
years. This avoids stale marking, but it creates survivorship and seasoning
bias. The current constituent list is not historical membership. Corporate
actions, dissemination timestamps, sector/correlation exposure, official
benchmarks, size-dependent impact, taxes, partial fills and confidence bounds
are also not yet complete.

Therefore these results may reject fragile strategies, but they cannot certify
a winner for live capital.

## Next research action

1. Stop spending experiment budget on combinations containing quarantined
   strategies.
2. Re-run the unchanged Minervini breakout baseline after point-in-time universe
   and corporate-action repair.
3. Compare it under identical rules with simple Indian momentum, quality, value
   and multi-factor baselines.
4. Research genuinely different mechanisms: quality-value, timestamp-correct
   post-earnings drift, volatility-controlled momentum, and a separately sized
   short-term reversal sleeve.
5. Allow only independently surviving families into portfolio combinations;
   then perform locked confirmation before any real-capital proposal.

Paper operation can continue as an execution-system test, but its returns must
not be treated as evidence that these strategies are fit for real money.
