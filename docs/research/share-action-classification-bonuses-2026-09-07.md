# Share-action classification: twelve bonus issues

Research date: 7 September 2026. Scope: bounded primary-source classification of AIIL, BSE, CUB, ECLERX, GODFRYPHLP, IRB, LALPATHLAB, MOTILALOFS, OIL, PHOENIXLTD, TARIL and TRENT, for events in 2024–2026 before the frozen snapshot end of 4 September 2026. No broker requests, price changes or executable quantity maps were made.

The company documents identify **bonus issues** for all twelve names. The total-share fractions below are arithmetic derived from the published entitlement terms, **not inferred from the observed Kite/raw volume ratios**. A bonus of A additional shares for B existing shares produces (A+B)/B total shares per previous share, before dealing with fractional entitlements. This classification does not establish each shareholder's credit date, complete action history, a cumulative vendor adjustment factor or historical security membership.

## Identity and implementation boundary

None of the classified notices establishes an old-ISIN/new-ISIN replacement caused by these bonuses. No transition is invented from an unchanged face value or a current instrument master. Where an ISIN is quoted above, it is only the identity actually stated in the cited filing. Establishing identity continuity for a dated executable map requires the relevant exchange/depository evidence and raw rows.

The observed fractions 4/3 and 3/2 especially cannot be passed directly into an API accepting only positive integer adjusted-unit increments. A rational share-coordinate model must define physical shares, adjusted units and fractional bonus entitlements, including the issuer's actual fractional-disposal/credit policy. Company announcements, allotment and tradability dates are separate evidence. Do not automatically credit bonus shares on a price ex-date, double-credit a backward-adjusted series, or treat agreement with an observed volume ratio as proof of complete unit coverage.

This document classifies specific events. It neither certifies that these were the only actions through 4 September 2026 nor authorizes broader unit maps, portfolio reruns, price repair, cash/entitlement credits or live trading. The explicit exchange ex-date gaps and the Motilal notice discrepancy remain open evidence items.
