# Proposed physical-share portfolio accounting policy

Research date: 2026-09-07. Scope: the full daily raw-price rerun, keeping the frozen strategy selection and risk configuration. This is a proposed research implementation contract, not a certification of returns, exact historical broker behavior, or tax accounting. No broker requests or code/data changes were made for this note.

## Initial runnable scope

The implementation lead reports that the legacy held intervals intersect dividends, ordinary AGM entries and one ZYDUSLIFE buyback, with no split/bonus/demerger crossings. That is an inspection finding supplied by the lead, not a guarantee that changed raw execution will produce identical holdings. Implement only the following initial accounting path; the general share-action details later in this note are deferred design guidance, not prerequisites when those actions are never encountered.

1. Fill and mark whole physical shares using verified raw daily prices and dated ticks. Retain separate signal/execution inputs and freeze all strategy settings.
2. Before ex-date opening trades, accrue each verified dividend to eligible prior holdings as a gross, pre-tax receivable. Never reuse it as buying power. Keep nominal stop and target prices unchanged for dividends. Preserve the receivable after the equity position closes.
3. Classify an ordinary meeting-only AGM record as no accounting entry. A combined AGM/dividend or other economic-purpose record still needs its economic event processed; a string containing “AGM” is not enough to ignore it.
4. Do not participate in the verified ZYDUSLIFE elective tender buyback. Do not create a buyback receivable, remove shares, or assume the tender price is a market fill.
5. On every actual held or newly admitted position, check the relevant dated action evidence. An unsupported mandatory action blocks the entire run, retaining an audit of the triggering position/event. Never resolve it by dropping that trade. Recheck during the new simulation rather than relying solely on legacy holding intervals.

ZYDUSLIFE's company Letter of Offer dated February 27, 2024 confirms a **tender offer**, record date **February 23, 2024**, at ₹1,005 per accepted share, open February 29 through March 6. Paragraph 20.6 explicitly allows shareholders to participate fully, partly, or not at all. This directly supports the nonparticipation policy for this particular event; it does not classify every record labeled “Buy Back.” [Company Letter of Offer, cover/schedule and paragraph 20.6, printed page 55](https://zyduslife.com/public/pdf/buyback2024/LetterofOffertoSE27022024.pdf).

Minimum additional state is therefore a dividend-entitlement ledger, an applied-event set, and separate economic equity versus buying power. Locked-share/fractional settlement engines need not be built before this bounded path can run: they become necessary only if an encountered action requires them. A successful result remains a research raw-accounting run under the declared signal, settlement and fee assumptions.

## What the primary sources establish

An ex-date starts trading without the corporate-action benefit. Under normal T+1 delivery settlement, buying before the ex-date establishes eligibility; buying on the ex-date does not. Record date and exchange ex-date must remain separate fields because holidays can affect their relationship. For simulation, taking the eligible holding snapshot immediately before ex-date opening trades is an inference from that convention, conditional on normal settlement; it is not evidence about failed deliveries in an actual account. [Zerodha eligibility explanation](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/ca-others/articles/what-does-ex-date-and-record-date-mean).

Dividends normally go directly to the shareholder's linked bank account. Zerodha does not establish the actual bank-credit timestamp; issuer/RTA evidence is needed for that. Thus entitlement, bank receipt, and money transferred back to the broker are different events. An announced payment deadline is not an observed credit. [Zerodha dividend payments](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/dividends/articles/i-hold-stocks-of-a-company-that-issued-dividends-how-and-when-will-i-get-the-dividends).

Bonus entitlement is not immediate availability for sale. SEBI's September 16, 2024 circular applies to bonus issues **announced on or after October 1, 2024**: record date T, deemed allotment next working day T+1, trading next working day T+2. It permits credit directly into the existing permanent ISIN. The rule explicitly anticipates delays and penalties, so its schedule is not proof that a particular account received tradable shares punctually. Do not apply it retrospectively to earlier announcements. [SEBI CIR/CFD/PoD/2024/122](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2024/1726484230463.pdf). The February 9, 2026 ICDR master circular retains this procedure in chapter 14; consolidation of the older circular does not remove the rule. [SEBI 2026 master circular, chapter 14](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/feb-2026/1770636136785.pdf).

Fractional entitlements are not exchange-tradable fractional shares. Issuer arrangements may pool and sell them and distribute cash. Neither rounding every account up nor silently discarding fractions is a general rule. Exact issuer terms and proceeds are required for settlement. [Zerodha fractional-share explanation](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/ca-others/articles/what-is-a-fractional-share), [bonus example](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/bonus/articles/impact-of-bonus-issue-on-equity-futures-options).

For splits, broker documentation describes a possible credit delay and limits sales to shares available in the account. A generic one-to-two-day estimate cannot establish historical availability for every security. Exchange trading under a new ISIN and the account's delivery availability are separate evidence questions. [Zerodha split explanation](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/stock-splits/articles/stock-split).

Existing GTTs cannot be assumed to survive actions unchanged: Zerodha lists split/bonus disablement and cancellation for certain other actions, including extraordinary dividends, and requires replacement of disabled orders. Any automatic bracket transformation in this simulator is our explicit replacement policy, not documented automatic broker behavior. [Zerodha GTT action handling](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/charts-and-orders/gtt/articles/gtts-disabled-cancelled-expired).

## Detailed semantics and deferred extensions

### 1. Two dated price views

Keep verified raw OHLCV immutable for fills, actual cash flows and daily marks. For a causal strategy evaluation, generate signals from a dated analytical view of raw history: at decision time t, only effective split/bonus events at or before t can rescale its earlier lookback into t's share units. Divide earlier prices by the verified total share multiplier and multiply corresponding volume. Never apply a later event to an earlier decision. Ranking and correlation require the same timing discipline; use actual raw turnover for liquidity evidence. A matched raw-accounting diagnostic may retain frozen vendor-derived signals to isolate accounting changes, but must disclose that this does not itself establish point-in-time signal validity.

Keep cash dividends out of this initial signal-price transformation and label the strategy a price-signal strategy with separately accounted distributions. A dividend-adjusted signal convention would be a separately frozen strategy variant. Reusing signals prepared from September 2026 Kite history does not establish what an earlier decision could observe. Kite's documented adjusted-chart coverage and unresolved cash-dividend mathematics are recorded in [the earlier primary-source note](kite-cash-dividend-conventions-2026-09-07.md).

### 2. Explicit state and event evidence

Maintain broker buying-power cash; unsettled sale proceeds if modeled; gross dividend receivables; received external-bank distributions; tradable integer shares; pending share claims; fractional claims; immutable purchase/sale cash flows; and per-event application identifiers. Use integer paise and exact rational ratios internally. Share-unit cost allocations must not rewrite historical purchase cash. Economic attribution and investor tax basis are separate concerns.

Each action needs a stable event identity, issuer/security lineage, event type, exchange ex-date, record date, announcement/publication evidence, source URL and body hash. Dividends require an explicit INR amount per eligible share and its share denomination. Share events require old/new units, an exact ratio and any published ISIN transition. Bonus new-to-old A:B means total multiplier (A+B)/B; split new-to-old A:B means A/B. Preserve combined actions as ordered events using issuer terms, not a ratio inferred from prices or volume. Tradability and fractional settlement need their own evidence/status.

The initial run requires actual held/admitted-position action checks throughout the new simulation. Certifying a causal signal evaluation additionally requires coverage of every security and lookback used by its candidate/ranking inputs: an unresolved event can alter ranking before appearing in a held position. Published action records need explicit classification of non-economic entries and corrections; unrecognized text must not silently become “no action.” Existing bounded event research is an input, not a complete action ledger. [Split classifications](share-action-classification-splits-2026-09-07.md), [bonus classifications](share-action-classification-bonuses-2026-09-07.md), [MAZDOCK/HEG lineage](mazdock-heg-share-lineage-2026-09-07.md).

### 3. Ex-date ordering and accounting

Before evaluating opening exits or admitting new entries, snapshot eligible pre-ex holdings and apply each effective event exactly once. An ex-date sale retains its entitlement; an ex-date purchase receives none for that event. Maintain claims after the original position closes.

For dividend D and eligible quantity Q, create gross receivable Q×D. Do not credit broker cash, reduce historical purchase cost, or change share quantity. Initial baseline policy: **no dividend reinvestment**. A missing actual payment date leaves an explicitly unsettled receivable; it cannot become buying power merely because a deadline passed. On verified receipt, move value from receivable to external-bank distributions once. This supports a gross accrual return, not a claim that the investor received that amount after withholding. Missing amount or eligibility date blocks even that accrual run.

Deferred if a share action is encountered: for split multiplier m, transform economic quantity by m and preserve aggregate purchase cash. Change the dated security identity where required. For bonus A:B, retain original shares and create a pending Q×A/B claim; do not pretend all resulting shares were tradable at ex-open. Aggregate entitlement at the beneficial account/security level before applying issuer fraction rules, then attribute it to strategy lots consistently. If three eligible shares earn a 1:2 bonus, preserve the 1.5 entitlement pending its documented whole/fraction treatment; do not round it away per trade. The initial implementation blocks such unsupported crossings instead.

Economic NAV includes cash, unsettled proceeds, marked tradable shares, supported marked pending share claims, gross dividend receivables and received external distributions exactly once. Marking a pending claim in the same fungible equity at raw close is a disclosed economic valuation assumption, not immediately realizable liquidation value. Report buying power, liquidatable holdings and pending claims separately. Unsupported fraction valuation/settlement or another resulting security cannot be hidden inside ordinary share quantity.

### 4. Tradability and pending exits

This extension is needed only when supporting a held share action, and is deferred for the initial runnable scope. Require a dated availability policy backed by event evidence. Do not equate split effective date, bonus ex-date, allotment date, exchange permission and actual account credit. A regulatory timetable may be used only as a clearly labeled research scenario; a verified-availability run must block where evidence needed for execution is missing.

If an exit fires while shares are unavailable, the minimal executable policy sells only the verified tradable quantity and records an irrevocable pending exit for the remainder. Sell that remainder at the first verified tradable session's raw opening execution price under the frozen slippage policy; never award the earlier stop price retrospectively. Apply this to timed exits too. Claims and residual risk survive closure of the saleable tranche. If this state transition is not implemented, block the affected full run rather than pretending the unavailable tranche filled or deleting the trade.

### 5. Stops, targets and settlement assumptions

Divide held stop/target prices by the verified split or bonus total multiplier before reading the ex-date bar. This preserves their share-unit meaning; it does not promise preservation of risk during an availability delay. Re-round replacement stop downward and target upward on the applicable historical tick, with the rounding policy declared. A present-day tick or retroactively adjusted chart increment cannot establish a historical trading tick. [Dated NSE tick evidence](nse-equity-tick-rules-2026-09-07.md).

For cash dividends, accounting does **not** uniquely determine a trading rule. To minimize unrequested strategy changes, the proposed first raw baseline keeps the existing nominal stop/target levels and includes dividend entitlement separately. This may trigger exits on an ex-dividend price fall and must be visible in the report. A distribution-neutral variant would subtract D from both brackets on the ex-date; that is an explicit strategy choice, not an inferred Kite adjustment or an equity exchange mandate. Freeze the choice before observing rerun performance. Do not silently apply a derivatives extraordinary-dividend threshold to these cash-equity stops.

The existing campaign reuses sale proceeds immediately. A full raw account must separately freeze a sale-settlement/buying-power convention. Conservatively delaying reuse until the next verified settlement day is a possible research policy, not a claim about all historical broker margin facilities. Missing required settlement calendars or action availability cannot be replaced with calendar-day arithmetic. This note does not establish historical settlement-credit rules.

### 6. Blocking and reporting

Unknown economic action terms affecting an actual held/admitted position, ambiguous required lineage, missing required raw prices or tick evidence, and unsupported mandatory share actions produce `DATA_BLOCKED` for the full requested run. Emit exact blockers and an audit checkpoint. Do not silently omit affected candidates through entry masks, drop trades, fabricate zero dividends, or publish a partial portfolio as the completed backtest. A declared simplified sale-settlement model is a limitation; certifying historical available cash additionally requires historical settlement evidence.

An unresolved dividend payment timestamp is narrower: with the declared non-reinvestment/accrual policy it need not prevent gross economic entitlement accounting, but it prevents an assertion of settled bank cash. Unsupported tax withholding similarly prevents net investor-return claims. Reports must distinguish these limitations from unknown entitlement amounts, which do block the economic result. Preserve the experiment's data/action hashes, signal convention, bracket policy, settlement model and cost-schedule version; successful accounting tests alone do not establish strategy profitability or live readiness.

## Required behavioral checks

For the initial scope, verify pre-ex ownership versus ex-date purchases; entitlement surviving an ex-date sale; dividend NAV accrual without buying-power credit; unchanged nominal dividend brackets; meeting-only events making no ledger entry; elective buyback nonparticipation; and deterministic blocking of unsupported held/admitted mandatory events. Receipt reclassification without a second gain is needed only when recording actual receipts. Later share-action support needs split cost/NAV invariance, delayed bonus tradability, stops during that delay, account-level fractions and correctly ordered combined actions. For a causal signal evaluation, add prefix invariance: future bars/actions must not change earlier signals, rankings or decisions. These tests do not certify source-event completeness or prove returns.
