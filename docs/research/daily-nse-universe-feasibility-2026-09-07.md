# Daily NSE liquidity universe: feasibility and limits

Research date: 7 September 2026. This is a bounded design assessment, not a universe reconstruction, strategy experiment, or data-admissibility certificate.

**A separately named, predeclared NSE stock liquidity universe is a viable research option without purchasing a historical Nifty 500 constituent anchor.** Its definition would depend on dated stock classification and lagged trading observations rather than index membership. Nifty 500 TRI can remain the external performance comparator: computing a comparison against an independently sourced index series does not require identifying its underlying constituents. This is a design conclusion, not a claim that the present files already establish a complete historical stock universe.

The new experiment must not relabel the existing 499-instrument experiment as survivorship-free or as historical Nifty 500. The unresolved anchor still matters whenever the requested universe is historical Nifty 500. The catalog contract already accepts a **named universe**, but its current implementation explicitly rejects raw/unadjusted bars pending corporate-action application. Changing the universe definition alone cannot remove that restriction. Sources: [catalog specification](../specs/point-in-time-market-data.md), [historical anchor investigation](nifty500-anchor-search-2026-09-07.md).

## Classification is the first unresolved dependency

The parser's `EQUITY_SERIES = {EQ, BE, BZ}` is an interim filter, explicitly not an authoritative security master. NSE's series legend groups fully paid equities **and ETFs** together, distinguishes SME series `SM/ST/SZ`, and also places rights entitlements in `BE`. REITs and InvITs have their own series. Therefore neither the broad cash segment nor the parser's “equity” label identifies ordinary main-board company shares. Sources: [parser and its classification warning](../../src/sensei/data/bhavcopy.py), [NSE series legend](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/legend-of-series).

The 2024-01-01 raw CSV provides a concrete counterexample: `BANKBEES`, ISIN `INF204KB15I9`, is `EQ` and `FinInstrmTp=STK`, yet is an ETF. Both sampled normalized sessions label it “equity.” Thus adding a `STK` check does not solve classification. Its source ZIP is [NSE's 2024-01-01 bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/cm/BhavCopy_NSE_CM_0_0_0_20240101_F_0000.csv.zip); locally captured ZIP SHA-256 is `067374fc5663cc4c0db1e4022e6e50de3491e3bbc7f209ec12fccbdafdd1fcfd`. The normalized parser retains symbol, series and ISIN but not the raw instrument name, financial-instrument ID, type or board-lot field. Retaining those fields would aid investigation; none is independently proven here to be an immutable stock identifier.

NSE publishes separate equity, ETF, SME, REIT and InvIT lists and symbol/name changes. Those are useful source leads, but a current list cannot be applied retrospectively. A first implementation needs **dated ordinary-stock classification**, dated SME/main-board migrations and exclusions of ETFs, REITs, InvITs, rights, preference shares, debt and other non-target instruments. Do not replace this evidence with symbol suffixes or one ISIN-prefix heuristic. A filename inventory did not locate a dated security-master archive in this repository; that limited search is not proof that no relevant evidence exists elsewhere. Source: [NSE securities available for trading](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/securities-available-for-trading).


## Proposed causal definition, to freeze before examining returns

This is a policy proposal, not implemented behavior. Define a universe such as “NSE main-board ordinary stocks meeting lagged liquidity and history requirements,” with a versioned rule fixing the lookback, turnover statistic and units, minimum history, inclusion threshold or rank count, rebalance cadence, tie-breaks and supported series. Threshold values remain a preregistration decision; this pass does not select or optimize them.

At session D's close, derive eligibility for the next execution session using only observations and classification evidence available by that decision. **Do not use the execution day's complete bhavcopy, turnover, or presence in that file to choose an opening trade.** A same-day file can validate a realized fill or reveal missing execution evidence; it cannot silently change the earlier candidate set. Publication dates and conservative availability timestamps belong in the manifest. Files retrieved now are frozen historical observations, not proof of what a historical subscriber received before subsequent corrections.

Build candidates from the union of historical observations and relevant dated security records. Do not intersect with today's Kite instruments, the surviving 499 stocks, or names that eventually acquire complete histories. A newly listed company may enter after its predeclared warmup and classification requirements are met; knowing later that it survives must not be an admission condition. The bhavcopy reader itself warns that a file describes securities **traded that day**, not all listed securities. Source: [reader's source contract](../../src/sensei/data/bhavcopy.py).

Daily turnover can support a lagged rupee-liquidity rule without mixing pre/post-split share volumes. Indicators still need independently verified causal action history; raw OHLC across a split is not a valid continuous signal series. Execution remains physical shares with raw prices, dated ticks and appropriate accounting. Neither turnover nor trading frequency alone certifies executable capacity or the chosen slippage estimate.

## Identity, missing observations and held positions

The minimum implementation requirements follow from the [stable-identity and coverage contract](../specs/point-in-time-market-data.md):

1. **Dated identity:** map symbol, series and ISIN to an internal stable instrument with sourced transition dates. ISIN can change after a split; symbol text can change or be reused. Preserve corporate successors separately where the economic instrument changes. Existing HEG/MAZDOCK case evidence is not an all-market lineage certificate.
2. **Reasoned absence:** distinguish source-file failure, malformed row, no trade, suspension, not-yet-listed, delisting and identity transition. First observed trading is not proof of listing date; last observed trading is not proof of delisting. An absent row does not authorize zero turnover, a fabricated OHLC bar, or a last-price exit.
3. **Observable selection versus missing evidence:** a predeclared trading-frequency rule may reject a stock because its past observed activity is insufficient. Unknown classification or incomplete files cannot be silently converted into an ordinary rule rejection. Block affected complete-universe claims, or explicitly label a narrower evidence-qualified experimental universe and its selection limitation.
4. **Held-position continuity:** failing a later liquidity screen prevents new entries under the rule; it does not delete an existing holding. A suspended/delisted holding needs an explicit valuation, pending-order and final-outcome policy supported by evidence. If a required execution bar or mandatory action is unresolved, the portfolio run must fail visibly rather than omit the trade or assume liquidation at the last close.
5. **Independent calendar and events:** verify the exchange-session calendar, each relevant source receipt, action completeness and dated tradability constraints. Detect identity duplicates before ranking. Empty or partial daily files must not shrink the universe unnoticed.

## Integration and decision

The existing quarantine reader and hashed ZIP/CSV/Parquet receipts are reusable ingestion evidence. The governed catalog's membership intervals can represent the **derived eligibility intervals of the newly named rule**, provided their provenance binds the exact rule, raw receipts, classification/identity evidence, calendar, availability convention and action policy. Those intervals must never be presented as index membership. `ADMISSIBLE`/point-in-time flags must not be switched on by caller assertion; the existing independently pinned manifest path and its raw-action restriction still apply. Source: [catalog specification](../specs/point-in-time-market-data.md), [bhavcopy implementation](../../src/sensei/data/bhavcopy.py).

Proceed first with a metadata-only prototype: locate dated stock classifications; reconcile identity and missing-session cases; choose and freeze either an extended raw warmup or the later start; then produce a deterministic eligibility manifest and causal acceptance tests. Only after action/physical-accounting coverage and the governed adapter are complete should a separate portfolio evaluation be run. Keep the independently sourced Nifty 500 **gross TRI** comparison on the same valuation dates, while clearly identifying portfolio costs, dividend treatment and the universe's different exposures. Changing the universe does not restore an untouched holdout in an already reused research period.

**Decision: viable alternative research route, not currently ready for an admissible portfolio run.** No historical index anchor is logically necessary for this custom rule, but authoritative dated classification, identity and missing-history coverage remain necessary. This pass neither guarantees that all such evidence is freely obtainable nor recommends a purchase. Four bounded official-domain search queries were used; there were no broker requests, bulk downloads, price experiments or code changes.
